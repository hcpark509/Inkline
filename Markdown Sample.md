# Weekend Reading Club

The club meets on **Saturday at 10:00 a.m.** Bring a favorite passage and *one question* to share.

### Before Saturday

- [x] Choose a passage to read aloud.
- [ ] Write down a question about a character.
- [ ] Bring a copy of the book.

## Discussion

1. Which detail did you notice first?
2. Where did your opinion of the narrator change?
3. What would you ask the author?

> Pick a passage that surprised you. Read it aloud before explaining why you chose it.
